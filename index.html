How connections are counted

A connection is a customer who has bought credit at least once. They count as active from their first payment to the end of the data, even through long gaps between payments, because the dataset has no record of any meter or customer being removed. Payments are taken as proof the meter was installed and working. Where the meter sent no readings, it is assumed to have been running offline on keypad tokens, not missing. On this basis most meters were installed in the first months, and the slow rise in reporting meters reflects years of work to bring meters online.

Day by day

The site-level series is measured at the data concentrator and starts in June 2022. It runs well above the sum of the meters (about 18% overall, up to 50% in 2022), then the two converge in September 2024, as the last offline meters started reporting. The dataset attributes the gap to an unmetered air conditioner and theft; under this page's assumption most of it is paid-for energy used by meters that were not yet reporting.

Data and method
About this data
  • Source: Kemabonta and Geoffrey (2026), A Multi-Year Daily Operations Dataset from a Rural Solar Mini-Grid in Southern Nigeria, CC BY 4.0, doi:10.5281/zenodo.24098636.
  • Connections are customers who have paid at least once, counted as active from their first payment onward; see "How connections are counted" at the top. This is an assumption: the dataset has no installation or removal records, and the customer-meter link is a snapshot with no history.
  • The currency switch converts naira to dollars at approximate yearly average rates (listed under Data and method; not part of the dataset). Each payment or cost is converted at its own year's rate. CAPEX uses the 2019 rate in the source table.
  • Payments are credit purchases (PAY records only). They are not the value of energy used, so price per kWh mixes the two.
  • Tariff change dates are approximate: NGN 165 to Jul 2023, NGN 350 from Jul 2023, about NGN 500 from about Feb 2024.
  • A zero meter-day can mean no use, a switched-off meter or no supply. Repeated identical readings rise to 12–14% of meter-days in 2023–24.
  • The per-meter table aggregates daily readings to months; it is built by scripts/build_meter_monthly.py.
  • No PV generation, battery, voltage or hourly data exists in the dataset.
Data: Kemabonta, T., & Geoffrey, A. (2026), doi:10.5281/zenodo.24098636, CC BY 4.0. Copies of the data files used here are in the data/ folder of this site's repository.
